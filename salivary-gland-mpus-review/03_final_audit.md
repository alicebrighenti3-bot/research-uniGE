performed by the same process that drafted the manuscript, under the network
constraints documented throughout; it is a structured internal consistency and honesty
check, **not a substitute for expert peer review or for the primary-source verification
pass required before submission** (see Section 0 of the evidence map, reiterated in
Section 17 of the manuscript).
